Who it’s for

  • Manufacturing and product-data teams comparing BOM exports before release
  • Engineering offices working from Excel, CSV or JSON BOM lists
  • Teams that export BOMs from PLM or ERP systems and need a clear local compare

Problems solved

Parts only on one side

See Source BOM only and Target BOM only rows instead of hunting two files by hand.

Hidden field differences

Different status highlights mapped fields that do not match, including quantity.

Unclear column mapping

Review which columns were compared and which were ignored before you trust the result.

Key capabilities

  • Upload Source BOM and Target BOM, then Validate BOMs
  • Summary filters: Matched, Different, Source BOM only, Target BOM only
  • Optional saved mapping presets and Mapping Manager when included in your Store licence
  • Search, column picker, row detail and ignored-item review
  • Export CSV or PDF when entitled; compare log download per Store licence
  • Runs locally on your Windows PC — BOM files are not uploaded to our servers
