OrderRelease Guard — catch RFQ, quote and PO differences before you release the job.

Manufacturers lose money when the purchase order is not the quotation that was accepted. OrderRelease Guard is a local Windows review workstation: it compares RFQ, accepted quote, customer PO and later revisions, shows source-linked discrepancies, and still requires a person to approve. It does not talk to your ERP or auto-enter orders.
